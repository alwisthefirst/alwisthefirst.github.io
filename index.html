Our Roots, Your Table

At Afrinaija Pots, we believe that food is the ultimate bridge between cultures. Born from a passion for authentic West African culinary craftsmanship, we brought the fiery spirit and rich traditions of Nigeria to the hills of Kigali.

We don't compromise. Our spices are sourced from the heart of Nigeria, and our techniques are passed down through generations. Whether you are a Nigerian longing for home or a Kigali local on a spicy adventure, our pots are open for you.
